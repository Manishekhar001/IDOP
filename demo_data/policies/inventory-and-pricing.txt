Northstar Outfitters — Inventory and Pricing Guidance (Synthetic Demo)
Effective date: 2026-01-01

Inventory counts below 8 units are considered low stock and should be reviewed for replenishment. A stock count must never be negative. Product price changes require a second-person review before they are applied to the catalog. The price shown in the product catalog is the current unit list price; it is not necessarily the amount paid on an order because orders may include multiple items, discounts, or shipping.

The demo schema does not contain product line items, supplier lead times, purchase orders, or a price-history table. Use it to find current low-stock products and explain the policy; do not infer which products were included in a particular order.
