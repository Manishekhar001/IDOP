Northstar Outfitters — Shipping and Order Status Guide (Synthetic Demo)
Effective date: 2026-01-01

Standard domestic orders are normally dispatched within 2 business days after payment clears. Delivery usually takes a further 3 to 5 business days. International orders may take 7 to 14 business days after dispatch, depending on customs and destination. A Processing order has not yet been dispatched. A Pending order is awaiting payment or order review. A Delivered order is marked as delivered by the carrier. A Cancelled order should not be treated as active sales.

These are general service expectations, not carrier tracking data. The demo order table records the order date and status, but does not contain dispatch dates, tracking events, payment state, or delivery timestamps.
