Northstar Outfitters — Returns and Refunds Policy (Synthetic Demo)
Effective date: 2026-01-01

Unused physical products may be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery when the customer can provide an order number. Items marked final sale are not eligible for a change-of-mind return. A product that arrives damaged should be reported within 7 calendar days of delivery with a description of the damage. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; allow 5 to 7 business days after approval for processing. Original shipping charges are refundable only when the company shipped the wrong item or the item arrived damaged.

This document describes general policy only. Eligibility for a specific order requires checking the actual delivery date, product condition, and item-level order details, which are not all represented in the demo database.
